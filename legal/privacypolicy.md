# Privacy Policy

This app is a local Discord Rich Presence (RPC) client. 

1. Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, account information, or listening history.
2. Data Transmission: All communication happens locally on your machine via Discord's local IPC socket to update your profile status. No data is sent to external servers.
3. Third-Party Services: This application interacts directly with the Discord API and is subject to the Discord Developer Policy.
